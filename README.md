# coffee-shop-sale-analysis
## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and transaction data from **three different
coffee shop** franchises using Microsoft Excel.

The goal was to explore revenue and transaction patterns across time,
product categories, and other business dimensions, and to identify
useful insights through pivot tables, charts, and interactive slicers..

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses different objectives:

* Analyze revenue and transaction trends over time
* Compare sales performance across the three franchises
* Identify peak transaction periods
* Analyze product category and product-type performance
* Create interactive visualizations for easier exploration
* Identifying the best-performing franchise
* Finding trends and patterns in the data

## 📁 Dataset

The analysis was performed using an Excel dataset containing information about three coffee shop franchises.
The dataset includes relevant business information such as:

* Transactions date, Time, and quantitties
* Transactions details by Months, Weekdays, per Hour
* Store locations (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Low Manhattan)
* Unit prices of the products
* Products_categories and products_types
* Product details
* Revenue

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Filters
* Formulas
* Pivot tables
* Line and Bar chart
* Slicers

## 🔍 Analysis

### 1. Time-Based Analysis

I analyzed revenue and transaction patterns across:

* Month
* Day of the week
* Hour of the day

This helped identify changes in customer activity and sales throughout
different periods.


### 2. Product Analysis

I explored transaction volume and revenue across different:

* Product categories
* Product types

This allowed me to identify which products contributed most to sales
and transaction volume.


### 3. Interactive Analysis

Slicers were added to allow the data to be filtered dynamically and make
the analysis more interactive.

### 📈   Visualizations
The analysis was presented using:

* Line charts to show trends over time
* Bar charts to compare categories and products
* Interactive slicers for filtering the analysis

## 💡 Key Findings

Some of the main insigits from the analysis were:

* Most of the revenue was being generated by Hell's Kitchen.
* At Astoria, peak time for business is 8-10am when every body is going to work, while, coffees and teas, especially the Brewed Chai tea and Gourmet brewed coffee, bring in most of the revenue. 
* At Hell's Kitchen, peak time for business is 8-10am when every body is going to work, and transactions mostly surged on Tuesday, Friday and sunday while, coffees and teas, especially the Barista Espresso and Brewed Chai tea, bring in most of the revenue.
* At Lower Manhattan, peak time for business is 7-10am when every body is going to work, and transactions mostly surged only on Mondays while, coffees and teas, especially the Barista Espresso and Gourmet brewed coffee, bring in most of the revenue. Here Least revenue is generated.
